\paragraph{Scope of the convergence result.}
Theorem~1 is conditional on Assumptions~2 and~3 for the sequence generated
by Algorithm~1. The numerical implementation uses approximate constrained
loading updates and enforces membership in $D$, but the sufficient-decrease
and relative-error conditions, together with the additional geometric
conditions used for the isolation and rate conclusions, have not been
verified for the implemented algorithm. Feasibility and numerical stopping
criteria alone do not establish these assumptions. Consequently, the theorem
does not provide an unconditional convergence guarantee for the numerical
implementation.
